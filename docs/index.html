Meet Your Teacher

Meet Anna

Anna Zhu is a pianist, MTNA-certified piano educator, and founder of Fly to the Moon Piano & Music Studio. She holds a master's degree in Communication and Public Relations from Johns Hopkins University.

Her teaching experience includes children and adults with different ages, learning styles, and musical goals, including children on the autism spectrum and older adults living with dementia.

Anna has completed online coursework focused on children with autism through the University of British Columbia and continues her own advanced piano studies with Victoria Bogdashevskaya, former principal pianist of the Seattle Symphony.

Her teaching brings together musicianship, communication, patience, and individualized learning so that every student can feel heard, challenged, supported, and inspired.

Teaching Philosophy

Music should be something students look forward to. Strong foundations matter, but so do curiosity, trust, communication, and the joy of discovering a personal musical voice.

☀

Joy Comes First

Lessons nurture a genuine love of music while gradually developing technique, musicianship, and confidence.

✧

Every Student Is Different

Some students learn by listening, some visually, and some through stories, movement, patterns, or questions. Teaching adapts to the learner.

♡

Communication Is Essential

Students are encouraged to say what they love, what feels difficult, and when they want to try another way. Their ideas matter.

↗

Growth With Purpose

Joyful learning still includes clear goals, thoughtful practice, and small challenges that build independence over time.

Weekly Moon Missions 🌙

Small, achievable missions may include a technique goal, listening activity, practice challenge, creative exercise, or musical discovery. The goal is not perfection — it is curiosity, consistency, and confidence.

Questions Are Always Welcome

Frequently Asked Questions

How does tuition payment work?

Tuition is accepted via Zelle or cash and is due at the beginning of each month. Monthly tuition reserves the student's regular weekly lesson time.

What is the cancellation and make-up policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Eligible make-up lessons are offered based on studio availability.

Are recitals and competitions available?

Studio recitals are offered twice each year. Students interested in competitions, auditions, examinations, or other performance opportunities are always welcome to discuss their goals.

Are master lessons available?

For selected students seeking advanced development, master lessons with university music professors or professional musicians may be available when appropriate.

What happens when the teacher is on vacation?

Planned studio vacations will generally be communicated at least one month in advance so families have time to plan.

Can parents contact the teacher about progress or practice?

Absolutely. Communication is an important part of learning. Questions about practice, repertoire, motivation, scheduling, or lesson goals are always welcome.
